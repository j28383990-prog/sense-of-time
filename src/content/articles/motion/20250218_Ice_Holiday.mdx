# Ferrari Purosangue

## Ferrari On Ice · Hulunbuir


2025年2月，法拉利将跃马带到了中国北方最辽阔的冰雪之境。

呼伦贝尔，这片位于中国北方边境的土地，在冬季被白雪覆盖，辽阔的草原、森林与冰封的河流共同构成了一幅独特的自然画卷。

而在这样的环境中，一场关于驾驶、探索、人车链接的 Ferrari On Ice 冰雪体验正式展开。


![Purosangue_29.jpg](/images/articles/motion/20250218_Purosangue/Purosangue_29.jpg)


远离城市的喧嚣，来到额尔古纳的冬日旷野。

这里没有传统赛道的边界，也没有城市道路的限制。

取而代之的是无尽的冰雪、极低的温度，以及对于驾驶者和车辆共同的考验。


![Purosangue_30.jpg](/images/articles/motion/20250218_Purosangue/Purosangue_30.jpg)


对于 Ferrari 而言，驾驶体验从来不仅仅关于速度。

从赛道到公路，再到极端环境下的冰雪路面，Ferrari 希望让驾驶者真正理解车辆的动态特性，并感受到人与机械之间最直接的交流。


![Purosangue_31.jpg](/images/articles/motion/20250218_Purosangue/Purosangue_31.jpg)


在这片北方土地上，一台来自意大利马拉内罗的 Ferrari Purosangue 开始展现它不同寻常的一面。

它不是为了取代传统 Ferrari，而是为了拓展 Ferrari Experience 的边界。


![Purosangue_37.jpg](/images/articles/motion/20250218_Purosangue/Purosangue_37.jpg)


冬季的呼伦贝尔拥有令人难忘的景色。

冰雪覆盖的大地、清澈的天空，以及远处延伸的地平线，让这次 Ferrari On Ice 体验不仅是一场驾驶活动，更成为一次独特的旅程。


![Purosangue_38.jpg](/images/articles/motion/20250218_Purosangue/Purosangue_38.jpg)


## Arrival in Yakeshi

抵达牙克石后，冰雪驾驶体验正式拉开序幕。

对于参与者而言，这不仅是一场车辆体验，也是一次深入北方冰雪文化的旅程。


![Purosangue_1.jpg](/images/articles/motion/20250218_Purosangue/Purosangue_1.jpg)


夜晚入住的酒店成为旅程中的第一站。

![Purosangue_2.jpg](/images/articles/motion/20250218_Purosangue/Purosangue_2.jpg)


零下数十度的气温提醒着所有人，这将是一场完全不同于日常驾驶环境的体验。

冰雪、低温以及极低附着力，将成为接下来驾驶过程中的最大挑战。


![Purosangue_3.jpg](/images/articles/motion/20250218_Purosangue/Purosangue_3.jpg)

# Ferrari Purosangue

## Ferrari On Ice · Hulunbuir


In February 2025, Ferrari brought the Prancing Horse to one of the most remote and breathtaking winter landscapes in China.

Hulunbuir, located in the northern part of Inner Mongolia, transforms into a vast white world during winter, where frozen rivers, snow-covered grasslands, and endless horizons create a unique natural stage.

Here, Ferrari On Ice began a special journey of driving, exploration, and connection between humans and machines.


![Purosangue_29.jpg](/images/articles/motion/20250218_Purosangue/Purosangue_29.jpg)


Far away from the noise of the city, we arrived at the winter wilderness of Ergun.

There were no traditional racing circuits and no limitations of urban roads.

Instead, there was only endless snow, extreme temperatures, and a completely different challenge for both driver and machine.


![Purosangue_30.jpg](/images/articles/motion/20250218_Purosangue/Purosangue_30.jpg)


For Ferrari, driving has never been simply about speed.

From the racetrack to public roads, and now to extreme environments such as icy surfaces, Ferrari continues to explore the relationship between the driver, the vehicle, and the surrounding world.


![Purosangue_31.jpg](/images/articles/motion/20250218_Purosangue/Purosangue_31.jpg)


On this northern land, the Ferrari Purosangue from Maranello revealed another side of its character.

It was not created to replace traditional Ferrari models, but to expand the possibilities of the Ferrari experience.


![Purosangue_37.jpg](/images/articles/motion/20250218_Purosangue/Purosangue_37.jpg)


The winter scenery of Hulunbuir is unforgettable.

The frozen landscape, clear sky, and endless horizon transformed Ferrari On Ice into more than a driving event — it became a unique journey.


![Purosangue_38.jpg](/images/articles/motion/20250218_Purosangue/Purosangue_38.jpg)
