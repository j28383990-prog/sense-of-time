# Ferrari 12 Cilindri&Purosangue

## A Ferrari Day

On May 17, 2025, Ferrari brought an experience centered around performance, design, and lifestyle to Hang Lung Plaza in Wuxi.

Under the theme of “A Ferrari Day”, the city became an extension of the Ferrari spirit.

![Ferrari Day](/images/articles/motion/20250517_FERRARI_DAY/FERRARI_DAY_1.jpg)

The event space was surrounded by Ferrari red, expressing the brand’s unique combination of passion and elegance.

![Ferrari Day](/images/articles/motion/20250517_FERRARI_DAY/FERRARI_DAY_2.jpg)

Entering the venue, the first model to capture attention was the Ferrari Purosangue.

As Ferrari’s first four-door, four-seat model, the Purosangue redefines the possibilities of a more versatile Ferrari.

It maintains the brand’s sporting DNA while offering a wider range of everyday usability.

![Ferrari Day](/images/articles/motion/20250517_FERRARI_DAY/FERRARI_DAY_3.jpg)

Guests experienced not only the cars themselves, but also the lifestyle philosophy behind Ferrari.

For Ferrari, a car has never been just a mechanical product, but a connection between culture and emotion.

![Ferrari Day](/images/articles/motion/20250517_FERRARI_DAY/FERRARI_DAY_4.jpg)

Several Ferrari models were displayed during the event, allowing guests to experience the personalities of different product lines up close.

The exhibition included the 296, Purosangue, and the new 12 Cilindri.

![Ferrari Day](/images/articles/motion/20250517_FERRARI_DAY/FERRARI_DAY_5.jpg)

Sitting behind the wheel, the familiar Ferrari driving atmosphere immediately returned.

The driver-focused cockpit creates a sense of ceremony every time one enters the car.

![Ferrari Day](/images/articles/motion/20250517_FERRARI_DAY/FERRARI_DAY_6.jpg)

The appearance of the 12 Cilindri once again highlights Ferrari’s dedication to the legendary V12 spirit.

![Ferrari Day](/images/articles/motion/20250517_FERRARI_DAY/FERRARI_DAY_7.jpg)

Every detail reflects Ferrari’s pursuit of exceptional materials, design, and craftsmanship.

![Ferrari Day](/images/articles/motion/20250517_FERRARI_DAY/FERRARI_DAY_8.jpg)

Even in an era of electrification, the emotion of a pure mechanical masterpiece remains irreplaceable.

![Ferrari Day](/images/articles/motion/20250517_FERRARI_DAY/FERRARI_DAY_9.jpg)

A Ferrari Day was not simply a vehicle exhibition, but an immersive experience of Ferrari culture.

From the 296 to the Purosangue and the 12 Cilindri, Ferrari continues to redefine passion through different expressions.
