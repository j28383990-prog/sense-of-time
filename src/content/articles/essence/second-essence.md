# First Essence


Essence is about the simple things that shape our daily experience.

![A classic machine](/images/articles/essence/first-essence.jpg)

A chair, a room, a piece of material, or a moment of silence can reveal a deeper sense of beauty.


The meaning of life is often hidden in ordinary details.
