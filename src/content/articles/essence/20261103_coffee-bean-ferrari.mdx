一只讨人喜欢的小家伙，一段充满 Ferrari 气息的日常。

在 Ferrari 展厅里，它自在得仿佛回到了自己的家。看看周围的经典跑车，找一个舒服的地方，然后毫无顾虑地倒头就睡。

而当主人开始维修经典 Ferrari 412 时，它又成为了最忠实的陪伴者。没有扳手，也不懂机械，却始终在主人身边，陪着每一个忙碌的时刻。

或许，它并不明白 Ferrari 412 的机械魅力，但它一定知道：只要陪在主人身边，在哪里都可以安心入睡。

A lovable little companion and everyday moments surrounded by Ferrari.

In the Ferrari showroom, this little friend feels right at home. Surrounded by beautiful classic cars, it finds a comfortable spot and simply drifts off to sleep, completely at ease.

When its owner gets to work restoring and maintaining a classic Ferrari 412, the little companion takes on another important role: the most loyal company in the workshop. No tools, no mechanical expertise, just a quiet presence by its owner's side through every moment of the work.

Perhaps it doesn't understand the engineering behind the Ferrari 412, but it certainly knows one thing: wherever its owner is, it's a good place to feel at home.

The sweetest companion. The most special Ferrari moments.
