## Oyster 的诞生：改变腕表历史的一刻

回顾 Oyster 的诞生，需要回到20世纪初。

当时，机械腕表最大的挑战之一，便是在面对水、灰尘以及复杂环境时，依然保持精准运行。

劳力士创始人 Hans Wilsdorf 坚信，腕表不应该只是精密的计时工具，而应该成为能够陪伴人们探索世界的可靠伙伴。

因此，防水性能成为劳力士长期研究的重要方向。

1926年，第一枚 Oyster 腕表正式诞生。

通过创新性的表壳结构设计，Oyster 成为了世界上首款真正意义上的防水腕表，也让腕表第一次拥有了面对日常环境挑战的能力。


![vintage](/images/articles/heritage/20260627_rolex-oyster-story/rolex-oyster-story_4.jpg)

## The Birth of Oyster: A Moment That Changed Watchmaking

To understand the birth of Oyster, we need to return to the early 20th century.

At that time, one of the greatest challenges for mechanical watches was maintaining precision while facing water, dust and unpredictable environments.

Hans Wilsdorf, the founder of Rolex, believed that a wristwatch should not only be an accurate timekeeping instrument, but also a reliable companion capable of accompanying people on their journeys.

Therefore, water resistance became one of Rolex's most important areas of research.

In 1926, the first Oyster watch was introduced.

Through its innovative case construction, Oyster became the world's first truly waterproof wristwatch, allowing watches to face everyday challenges while maintaining reliability.

![vintage](/images/articles/heritage/20260627_rolex-oyster-story/rolex-oyster-story_4.jpg)

## A Century of Technical Heritage

Over the following century, Oyster became the foundation of many legendary Rolex collections.

From Oyster Perpetual to Datejust, Submariner and Daytona, each model continues the values of reliability and innovation established by Oyster.

The historical timepieces displayed in the exhibition allow visitors to experience how watch design and technology evolved across different eras.

Behind every watch lies a unique understanding of precision, durability and aesthetics.

![detail](/images/articles/heritage/20260627_rolex-oyster-story/rolex-oyster-story_5.jpg)

The value of a mechanical watch is never defined only by its complex structure.

For Rolex, watchmaking is not only a technical achievement, but also a continuous pursuit of excellence.
